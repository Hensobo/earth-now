# Code of Conduct

Earth Now follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

We want this to be a friendly, welcoming project for everyone – regardless of age, body size,
visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

**Please do:** be kind and patient, assume good intentions, give and accept constructive feedback,
and focus on what's best for the community.

**Not okay:** sexualized language or imagery, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or
political attacks, public or private harassment, publishing others' private information without
their explicit permission, and any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate
in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions
that don't follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for
behavior they deem inappropriate.

To report a problem, use GitHub's [report content](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
feature or contact the maintainer privately via a
[private report](https://github.com/Hensobo/earth-now/security/advisories/new). All reports are
handled confidentially.

The full text of the Contributor Covenant 2.1, including the enforcement guidelines, applies and is
available at <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.
