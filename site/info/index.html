Privacy and credits

Your data stays on your Mac. The pictures come from NASA and weather satellites.

The app

Earth Now collects no data. No analytics, no accounts, no update checks in the background. It goes online for exactly two things: the cloud map from clouds.matteason.co.uk every few hours, and, only when you search for a place, your search text to Apple Maps. Like any web request, those servers see your IP address.

This website

No cookies, no trackers, no external fonts or scripts. Everything is served from GitHub Pages; GitHub may log your IP address for security (see GitHub’s privacy statement). The live Earth on the home page loads its cloud map from clouds.matteason.co.uk.

The clock and the colour of the home page are worked out in your browser from your time zone setting. Your location is never asked for and nothing is sent anywhere.
